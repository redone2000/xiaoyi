Pop Blocks Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Pop Blocks is a casual puzzle game developed by RedOne. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information.

Feedback and Replies

If you choose to submit feedback, Pop Blocks sends your selected feedback category, message, app version, and build number to our feedback service. For each submission, the app creates a random receipt ID and a separate random reply token. The service stores the receipt ID and a one-way hash of the reply token, and may store our reply and the time of that reply. We use this information only to receive, review, and respond to feedback and to operate and protect this feature.

Pop Blocks does not send your Apple ID, device model, advertising identifier, game progress, diamond balance, purchase history, or contact information with a feedback submission. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive personal information in your feedback message.

Records on Your Device

Pop Blocks stores up to 20 anonymous feedback receipt records on your device so that you can check for replies. A record may include the feedback category, submission time, receipt credentials, reply status, reply text and time, and read state. The submitted feedback message itself is not stored in this local receipt list. These local records are removed when you uninstall the app and cannot be restored because Pop Blocks does not use an account for feedback.

Service Operations, Sharing, and Tracking

Our hosting, security, and operational notification providers may process feedback and ordinary technical request information as needed to deliver, protect, and notify us about this feature. We do not sell feedback, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

Retention and Deletion

Feedback, replies, and related service records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to review and respond to feedback, maintain the feature, prevent abuse, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Because feedback is anonymous, we may need the approximate submission time, category, app version, and a distinctive part of the message to locate a record. You may request deletion through the support page below. We will delete a record we can reasonably identify unless we must retain it for security or legal reasons.

Game Center

If you use Game Center features such as leaderboards, Apple may process your Game Center account information according to Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID password or payment information.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide aggregated crash or performance information to help developers improve apps. We use this information only to diagnose and improve app stability.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us through the support page: https://redone2000.github.io/xiaoyi/popstar.html

Pop Blocks 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 28 日

Pop Blocks 是 RedOne 开发的一款休闲益智游戏。本隐私政策说明本 App 如何处理信息。

反馈与回复

如果你选择提交反馈，Pop Blocks 会将你选择的反馈类型、反馈内容、App 版本号和构建号发送到我们的反馈服务。每次提交时，App 会分别生成随机的回执编号和回复令牌。服务端保存回执编号及回复令牌的单向哈希，并可能保存我们的回复内容和回复时间。这些信息仅用于接收、查看和回复反馈，以及运行和保护该功能。

Pop Blocks 不会随反馈发送你的 Apple ID、设备型号、广告标识、游戏进度、钻石余额、购买记录或联系方式。请勿在反馈内容中填写密码、验证码或其他敏感个人信息。

本机记录

为了让你查看回复，Pop Blocks 最多在这台设备上保存 20 条匿名反馈回执。回执可能包含反馈类型、提交时间、回执凭据、回复状态、回复内容与时间以及已读状态。本地回执列表不会保存你提交的反馈正文。卸载 App 后这些本机记录会被移除；由于反馈功能不使用账号，记录无法恢复。

服务运行、分享与跟踪

为了提供、保护该功能并向我们发送运营提醒，我们的托管、安全和运营通知服务商可能会在必要范围内处理反馈及常规技术请求信息。我们不会出售反馈，不会将反馈用于广告，也不会利用反馈跨 App 或网站跟踪你。

保留与删除

反馈、回复及相关服务记录仅在查看和回复反馈、维护功能、防止滥用、履行法律义务及处理争议所合理需要的期限内保留。由于反馈是匿名的，我们可能需要大致提交时间、反馈类型、App 版本和反馈内容中的显著片段来定位记录。你可以通过下方支持页面申请删除。对于能够合理定位的记录，除非因安全或法律原因必须继续保留，我们会予以删除。

Game Center

如果你使用排行榜等 Game Center 功能，Apple 可能会根据其隐私政策处理你的 Game Center 账户信息。我们不会获取你的 Apple ID 密码或支付信息。

App 内购买

App 内购买由 Apple 处理。我们不会获取或存储你的银行卡等支付信息。

诊断信息

Apple 可能会向开发者提供汇总后的崩溃或性能信息，用于帮助改进 App 稳定性。我们仅将这些信息用于问题诊断和体验改进。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，可通过支持页面联系我们：https://redone2000.github.io/xiaoyi/popstar.html
