Desk Memory Privacy Policy

Updated: October 24, 2026 · Free, on-device macOS app. No account required.

Scope

This policy applies to the free, account-free macOS version of Desk Memory. We do not operate servers for audio or transcripts, display advertising, or include analytics or tracking SDKs.

Recording & Consent

The app accesses your selected microphone only after you start recording, enable a scheduled automatic action, or start an input test. It explains the purpose and requests microphone permission before capture, and continuously shows recording status in the menu bar. Closing the main window does not stop recording; you can stop it from the menu bar. Quitting stops recording and saves the final segment. Recovery after a brief system interruption follows your existing recording request. Relaunching the app does not start recording automatically. Before recording other people, inform them and obtain any consent required in your circumstances.

On-Device Processing

Audio, transcripts, device selection, recording times, schedules, and diagnostics are stored in this Mac’s app sandbox. Apple SpeechTranscriber transcribes on device. The app does not upload audio or text or send content to third-party AI services such as DeepSeek. macOS may download Apple-managed models when preparing speech recognition; this does not upload your audio. The app itself has no network client entitlement.

Retention

Text and history are kept until you delete them. The app normally checks saved, successfully transcribed audio every minute. From each recording’s end time, audio older than 10 minutes is compressed locally to AAC and fully decoded for verification before replacing the uncompressed original. Audio older than 1 hour is deleted, including compressed copies. If compression fails, the original is retained until the same one-hour expiry. Pending, failed, and unrecovered audio is not automatically deleted, to avoid loss. Input test recordings also require manual deletion. Diagnostics remain local and include runtime events, times, device identifiers, and errors; they are not sent automatically. Each log rolls over at approximately 512 KB. Schedules and device preferences remain until you change or delete them.

Your Controls & Deletion

You can stop recording at any time, withdraw recording consent in Settings > Privacy & Data, or revoke microphone access in macOS System Settings. Delete All Audio & Transcripts first stops capture and transcription, then removes this app’s audio, transcripts, legacy summaries, recording index, and content diagnostics. Device selection, schedules, and consent preferences remain. If deletion fails, a pending-deletion marker is retained and new recording is blocked until deletion completes after a restart or retry. Deletion does not affect copied or exported content, the original legacy folder, system backups, copies in other apps, or shared speech models managed by macOS.

Copying, Importing & Notifications

Content goes to the system clipboard or a location you choose only when you copy or export it. Universal Clipboard, backups, and receiving apps are subject to their own settings and policies. Legacy import copies only the folder you select and leaves its original contents intact. Remove the original folder and backups yourself if you no longer need them. Reminders use local system notifications, without our own push server. macOS can deliver scheduled notifications after the app quits.

Security & Changes

The sandbox limits file access; it does not add separate encryption to the app’s database. Use macOS account security, screen locking, and FileVault for system protection. You can view saved data and this policy without granting microphone access. We will update this policy if data processing changes. New recording or sharing purposes will be explained separately and require appropriate consent.
